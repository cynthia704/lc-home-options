About Victor

Victor Pierantoni, Liberation Coach

Victor Pierantoni is the coach behind Liberation Coach, offering 1:1 coaching for founders, business owners and adult athletes. His background includes martial arts and an interest in how Zen and Stoic philosophy relate to daily life.

His coaching considers both the situation a person faces and how that person responds to it.

Martial Arts, Zen and Stoicism

Victor's public biography includes martial arts and founding Zen Stoic. In his public interviews, he discusses attention, stillness, emotional experience and the questions people bring to their lives.

That background gives context to his coaching: the conversation can look at a practical decision and the feelings or assumptions surrounding it. The aim is to connect what you notice with a choice you can make.

A Real Situation, Examined More Closely

A decision can involve both the facts in front of you and the meaning you give them. Victor's reflective questions examine what happened, what you believe it means and which action you want to consider next. His emotional-awareness teaching also pays attention to bodily sensations associated with a feeling.
